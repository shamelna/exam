Congratulations on completing the learning modules and project coaching. This rigorous examination validates your mastery of lean principles, the Toyota Production System, Value Stream Mapping and Scientific Problem Solving.

Exam Overview

Questions 180 multiple choice
Time Limit 2 hours
Passing Score 70% (126/180)
Attempts Included 2 attempts
Retake policy: Two attempts are included with your programme. If you need an additional attempt, an exam voucher must be purchased. Allow 48 hours between attempts.
Important: Once started, the exam cannot be paused. The two-hour timer runs continuously. Confirm a stable internet connection and a quiet environment before beginning.
